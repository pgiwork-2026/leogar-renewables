Who we are

Leogar Renewable Private Limited is a manufacturing and technology-based company committed to innovation, technical excellence and skill development.

Our company focuses on technology development, manufacturing activities and practical industrial training. We work with educational institutions to provide industry-oriented training programmes and internship opportunities that help students bridge the gap between academic learning and industrial requirements.

Through our technical expertise and practical learning approach, we aim to contribute to technological advancement and develop a skilled, industry-ready workforce.
